## Decisions / findings (worth remembering)

1. **The capacity model has no concurrency dimension.** The SOP sums serial effort against one number; Ethan runs ~3 sessions. The session reinterpreted `Weekly capacity days` as session-days (15) with `Tier effort days: Quick 0.1, Standard 0.5, Heavy 1`. Works, but it quietly redefines the key. Either document session-days semantics in `method.config.template.md` or add a `Parallel sessions` key. The defaults (6 days, 0.5/2/4) were 4–5× too large for this user.
2. **The Grounding scout's verdict was inverted.** It verified the two comfortable sinks (`menu.js:180`, `:389`, which have the PIPER-484 CHECK fallback) and missed the outward one (`project-detail-main.js:2333`, `item.unit` raw, no constraint by design). "No fallback" survived because the briefing led with it; the reason was wrong. Scout prompt needs: read the sink in code, name the fallback's file and line, state which sink is outward-facing.
3. **A dependent's in-flight draft must pull its blocker into the candidate set.** 1167's three unpushed commits were flagged as a leak while 1154, its Hard blocker, went unscheduled.
4. **Themes cross lanes and the lane read cannot see them.** Ethan's "Eval Loop" spanned I8.P2 and I5.P1. Let the user name themes as briefing input, or derive candidates from labels/keywords alongside lane heads.
5. **The briefing is unreadable without titles.** Ethan: "i don't know what they all are by name." The session adopted titles beside IDs on request; make it the template, in the briefing, the read-back and the `[tpm]` comments.
6. **Ask questions one at a time.** Three bundled questions → Ethan missed the third ("i totally missed that"). Phase 3 should surface one question per turn, each with options and a pick, even in default mode.
7. **Gloss the jargon.** "Leaks", "floors", "criterion 4", "not changing" all needed a plain-language sentence. The read-back's "not changing" list is the right control for a 28-write phase, but only if the user knows what it is for.
8. **Read-back posted with a blank WAIT cell.** The 640 fallback arrived after the read-back. Rule: no read-back until every WAIT row has its fallback, or mark the row as unverified in the block itself.
9. **Config rows landed in the wrong table.** The session appended `Weekly capacity days` / `Tier effort days` under `### Area Labels` (two-column table). `pk_config` greps `| **Key** |` anywhere, so it reads them; the skill should name the key table.
10. **Reviewer's own misses.** I said "your five don't fit the week" (wrong once capacity was measured in his units), "1154 looks like it carries a migration" (wrong; piper-only), and earlier "no lane had a focus tier" (`pk project list | head -30` cut off the I8 lanes). Measure capacity in the user's units before judging fit; never truncate a listing you are about to reason over.
11. **Candidate-set rule "anything Urgent/High" is unbounded on this board** (52 High, 26 of them in Needs Spec). It needs "in a ready state" or a cap; the skill coped by reasoning from lane heads + in-flight, which is what the rule should say.
12. **The ship-path claim was right.** For Game7, "ships" is live on app.withpiper.ai: SiteLine merge → piper `/sync-legacy` (PR to dev) → promote dev→beta→main. The legacy `deploy-prod.sh` hop to app.siteline.pro is additional, not user-facing. Verified against SiteLine `method.config.md` L173–196 and piper's sync-legacy skill.
