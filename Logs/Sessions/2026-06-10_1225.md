2. **The `--help` bug was worse than reported**: all seven pk subcommands executed on `--help` — `pk next --help` actually queried Linear. 4. **"Intended drift signal" on constitutional docs is how the GUIDE drift survived rc3** — a stale stamp is indistinguishable from the convention. Release checklist now stamps method/RUNBOOK/GUIDE unconditionally; an unedited bump asserts re-check.
5. **`pk promote`'s "hardcoded --base main" (Piper finding #8) is already fixed upstream** (v2.6.0+ derives source/target from Ship environments) — the report came from Piper's old v2.7.1 copy. Multi-hop-in-one-command remains open, but smaller than believed.
